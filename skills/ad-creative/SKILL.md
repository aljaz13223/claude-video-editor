# ad-creative

1. **Founder/person page** often beats the brand page — a face and a name build trust.
2. **Trojan story**: raw phone photo/video + a real story (struggle → turn → result); the product appears late.
3. **Slippery lead-in**: the first 11–14 words jump into the action or the peak, conversational, often in quotes
   ("I got 72 leads in 5 days and…"). This one change moves results more than anything else.
4. **News/gossip format**: big specific benefit + burning curiosity. Check: breaks the pattern? opens a loop? specific benefit?
5. **Soft CTA** ("Learn more" / "Preberi več") over hard sell.
6. **Barbell**: either truly raw or truly premium — generic middle-quality UGC performs worst.
7. **Testing order** when an ad fades: new creatives first (many statics/variants), then headlines, then body text.
   Judge on 7-day averages; frequency < 3 means the audience is not tired yet. Don't kill an ad before new creatives are live.
8. **Formats**: 9:16 (Reels/Stories, keep text out of the top 14 % and bottom 20 %), 4:5 feed, 1:1 optional.
   Captions always on (most watch muted); speech starts at once.

Deliver per ad: video/static in each format, 3 lead-in variants, 3 headlines, 2 body texts, the CTA — in one table.
Never fabricate testimonials, numbers or reviews; use only what the client can prove.
